Buying Guide

How to Choose the Right Laptop in Nigeria

By TechPick NG • May 10, 2024 • 8 min read

Buying a laptop can be confusing. This guide will help you understand the most important things to consider before you buy.

1. Determine Your Needs

What will you mainly use the laptop for? Your needs will determine the type of laptop you should buy.

📚

Students

For school work, research and browsing.

💼

Office Work

For productivity, documents and meetings.

🎨

Content Creation

For editing photos, videos and designing.

🎮

Gaming

For high-performance games.

2. Key Specifications to Look For

Here are the main specs explained in simple language:

Component What to Look For
Processor (CPU) Intel Core i3, i5, i7 or AMD Ryzen 3, 5, 7 (newer is better). Higher numbers usually mean stronger performance.
RAM 8GB minimum (16GB recommended for smooth performance and multitasking).
Storage SSD is much faster than HDD. 256GB is a good start; 512GB or more is better if you store many files.
Display Full HD (1920×1080) is ideal. Larger screens are nicer but less portable.
Battery Look for at least 6–8 hours of claimed battery life if you work away from power often.
Graphics Integrated graphics are fine for office and browsing. Dedicated GPU is better for gaming and heavy video editing.
